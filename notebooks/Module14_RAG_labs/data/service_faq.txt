Sunrise Demo Bank - Fictional training FAQ
All policies are synthetic. No customer data is included.

Q: What should I do if my debit card is lost?
A: Block the card immediately using the mobile banking app or contact the demo support desk. Request a replacement card; the replacement charge is INR 150.

Q: How do I update my address?
A: Submit new address proof and a signed address change request at the branch. Processing takes three working days after verification.

Q: How long does personal loan processing take?
A: Three working days after all required documents are received and verified. Approval is not guaranteed.

Q: How do I raise a service complaint?
A: Submit a complaint in the mobile banking app or at a branch. Initial acknowledgement is within one working day and target resolution is seven working days.
